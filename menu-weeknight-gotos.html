Nick + Sarah + Wilson — Summer 2026

Weeknight Gotos

Five dinners that work when you're tired, the baby's fussy, and you still want to eat well

≤45 minutes active · One pan or one pot · Zero nuts · No bullshit

⚠️ Allergies — Read Every Label

Nick & Sarah: NO PEANUTS, NO TREE NUTS — in any form. No almond flour, no walnut oil, no cashew cream, no Nutella, no pine nuts, no pesto with nuts. Check every condiment, every sauce, every "may contain" statement.
Nick: Also avoids lentils and peas (all types).
Sarah: Milk allergy — cheese is fine (feta, mozzarella, parmesan, blue cheese, etc.), but no cream sauces, no milk-based soups, no dairy-heavy desserts. Olive oil and plant butter work for her swaps.
Wilson: 6 months old — starting solids. These recipes intentionally include components that mash well.

🥣 The Weeknight Strategy

Every dinner here follows the same philosophy: one pan or one pot, minimal cleanup, big flavor. Most are done in 30-40 minutes flat. A few benefit from Sunday batch cooking (Bolognese, soups) — make a double batch and you've got Wednesday covered too. Each meal has a Wilson-friendly component: something soft, simple, and unseasoned that mashes easily.

Monday · 40 Minutes

Crispy Chicken Thighs + Pan Sauce

The most reliable dinner in the rotation. Bone-in skin-on thighs, roasted until the skin shatters, with a 3-minute pan sauce that makes it feel like a restaurant meal. Sweet potato wedges roast alongside on the same pan.
The Main Event 50 min · 10 active
Four bone-in skin-on chicken thighs, seasoned and roasted at 425°F for 35 minutes, then broiled 2-3 minutes for crackling skin. Sweet potato wedges on the same sheet pan, tossed in oil, salt, smoked paprika. While the chicken rests, deglaze the pan with white wine, shallot, garlic, chicken stock, and mount with cold butter. Takes more time than effort — the oven does the work.
Wilson: Mashed sweet potato + finely shredded dark meat. A winner every time.

Tuesday · 35 Minutes

Sheet Pan Sausage + Veggies

One pan. Zero fuss. Italian sausage, kale, asparagus, cherry tomatoes, garlic — all on one sheet pan at 425°F for 20-25 minutes. The juices from the sausage season everything. Finish with lemon and parmesan.
The Sheet Pan 35 min · 10 active
Italian sausage links (sweet or spicy), kale (stems removed, torn), asparagus (woody ends snapped), cherry tomatoes, smashed garlic. Toss in olive oil, salt, pepper, fennel seeds. Roast 20-25 minutes at 425°F. Finish with lemon slices and fresh basil. The kale gets crispy at the edges, the tomatoes burst, and the sausage renders into everything.
Wilson: The softest sausage chunk + torn kale + mashed tomato. All on one tray.

Wednesday · 15 Minutes

Shrimp Ajillo

The fastest dinner in the rotation. Shrimp, garlic, olive oil, chili flake — sizzling in a skillet for 3 minutes. Serve with crusty bread for sopping the oil. It's faster than ordering takeout.
The Skillet 15 min · 10 active
1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined. Pat dry (wet shrimp don't sear). Heat 1/3 cup olive oil in a skillet with 6 cloves thinly sliced garlic and a pinch of chili flake — just until the garlic starts to sizzle. Add shrimp in a single layer. Cook 90 seconds per side. Finish with lemon juice, parsley, flake salt. Serve with crusty bread for the oil.
Add a quick salad or steamed green beans to round it out. Wilson: mashed avocado + a soft shrimp piece.

Thursday · 15 Minutes (if prepped)

Bolognese + Pasta

This one rewards Sunday afternoon. Make a double batch of Bolognese in 90 minutes, freeze half, eat the rest Thursday. Reheating takes the time the pasta takes. Comfort in a bowl — and Wilson can eat the pasta soft.
Batch Day (Sunday) 90 min · 30 active
The real Bolognese — not a weeknight shortcut. Ground beef + pork, soffritto (onion, carrot, celery), white wine, whole milk, San Marzano tomatoes. Cook low and slow, 60 minutes, until the milk and wine are absorbed and the sauce is thick and velvety. Freeze in 2-serving portions. Thursday night: boil pasta (linguine or pappardelle), reheat sauce in a wide pan, toss, serve with parmesan.
Wilson: Soft pasta pieces with a little sauce. Big hit.

Friday · 25 Minutes

Chicken Cutlets

Friday deserves something that feels like a reward but takes as long as a weeknight. Thin-sliced chicken breast, breaded and pan-fried in oil until golden and shattering. Sandwich it, salad it, or just eat it with lemon and a vegetable.
The Cutlet 25 min · 20 active
Pound chicken breasts to even 1/4-inch thickness. Season hard with salt and pepper. Dredge in flour, then egg, then seasoned breadcrumbs (add garlic powder, oregano, paprika to the crumbs). Fry in 1/4 inch of oil at 350°F — 3 minutes per side, until deep golden. Drain on paper towel. Serve with lemon wedges and whatever green thing is in the fridge — arugula with lemon vinaigrette, sautéed spinach, or roasted broccoli.
Make extra + turn into Chicken Parm the next night. Wilson: thin shreds of cutlet + soft vegetable.

The Next 5

Rotate These In

Not every week needs to be the same five. Swap any night for these — they follow the same rules: fast, one-pan or one-pot, nut-free, Wilson-approved.
Shiitake & Kale Fried Rice 20 min
Leftover rice + shiitakes + kale + egg + soy + sesame oil. Come home, make rice. Actually use leftover rice from the night before — fresh rice is too wet. The key is high heat and not crowding the pan. Scramble an egg, push it aside, fry the mushrooms, add the kale, toss the rice through. Finish with scallions and sesame seeds.
Wilson: The soft scrambled egg + well-cooked rice. Skip the soy.
✅ Nut-free · ✅ Sarah: dairy-free · ✅ Nick: no lentils/peas
Burger Bowls 25 min
No bun, no problem. Good ground beef (80/20) formed loosely, seasoned hard with salt and pepper, seared in a cast iron skillet 4 minutes per side. Rest it. Slice it. Serve over romaine with tomato, red onion, pickles, and your own dressing — a quick buttermilk ranch for Nick, tahini-lemon for Sarah. A burger that doesn't need a bun and leaves you lighter.
Wilson: Finely minced burger + mashed avocado. Protein and fat he needs.

Miso-Glazed Salmon 25 min
Whisk white miso, sake (or mirin), soy sauce, a touch of sugar. Coat salmon filets. Broil 8-10 minutes — the glaze caramelizes, the fish stays medium-rare at the center. Serve with steamed rice and a quick shiitake-kale sauté. The miso is great for Nick's gut protocol.
Wilson: Flaked salmon + well-mashed rice. Omega-3s for the baby brain.

Black Bean & Chorizo Tacos 20 min
Brown chorizo (casings removed) in a skillet. Add a can of black beans (drained), cumin, chili powder, a splash of water. Smash some beans with the back of a spoon to thicken. Warm corn tortillas direct on the burner. Fill with the chorizo-bean mix, diced onion, cilantro, crema (or lime-smashed avocado for Sarah), hot sauce. A 20-minute taco night that beats delivery.
Wilson: Mashed black beans + avocado. This is going to be a favorite.

Mediterranean Sheet Pan Chicken 40 min · 10 active
Chicken thighs (boneless or bone-in) on a sheet pan with red onion wedges, bell peppers, cherry tomatoes, chickpeas, olives. Toss everything in olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic, salt, pepper. Roast at 425°F for 30 minutes (boneless) or 35-40 (bone-in). The chickpeas get crunchy, the tomatoes burst, the chicken stays juicy. Feta on top at the end (hold for Sarah).
Wilson: Mashed chickpeas + soft pepper + finely shredded chicken. Pre-loaded with flavor.
✅ Nut-free · ✅ Sarah: hold feta, otherwise dairy-free · ✅ Nick: no lentils/peas
