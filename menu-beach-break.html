Venerdì Pomeriggio · 4 PM

Antipasti from the Oven

These hit the oven from 4 PM onward while the oven gets up to pizza temp. Nothing takes more than 4 minutes. Pass pans around family style. Make the eggplant first — it's the showstopper.

Blistered Shishito Peppers
Tossed in oil and salt, spread on a sheet pan, 2-3 minutes in the oven. They blister and pop. Squeeze lime, hit with flake salt. Zero effort, big payoff.
✅ Everyone · GF · Vegan
Charred Caesar
Romaine hearts halved lengthwise, brushed with oil, laid directly on the oven floor for 60-90 seconds. Cut side chars, interior stays cool and snappy. Garlicky Caesar dressing, crispy breadcrumbs. A completely different vegetable than raw romaine.
Sarah's: hold the parmesan · Sarah Bianco's: GF breadcrumbs
✅ Livi
Roasted Eggplant — Honey & Tahini
Thick eggplant planks, oiled, salted. Sheet pan in the oven until collapsing and deeply browned — 5-6 minutes. Finish with tahini, honey, flake salt, torn mint. Serve warm or at room temp. People who don't like eggplant love this.
✅ Everyone · GF · Vegan
Pizza Oven Broccolini
Tossed in olive oil, salt, chili flake. Spread on a sheet pan, 3-4 minutes. Tips get dark and crispy, stalks stay tender. Squeeze lemon, shower with pecorino (hold for Sarah — just lemon + salt).
✅ Everyone · GF
Sweet Potato Wedges
Cut into wedges, tossed in oil, smoked paprika, salt. Oven ambient heat (not the floor) — 8-10 minutes. Edges caramelized and crispy. Garlic aioli for dipping.
✅ Everyone · GF · Vegan (if aioli is omitted or vegan)

Sabato Sera · Pesce

Boys' Seafood Dinner

What we caught determines what we cook. If we skunked, the fish market backup plan is locked and loaded. All prep happens while the sun goes down.

🎣 If We Catch Fish —
Whole Grilled Striper or Fluke
Score the skin. Stuff the cavity with lemon, garlic, herbs. Grill 6-8 minutes per side over medium heat. Skin crackles, meat stays juicy. Bring it to the table whole — it's a show. Serve with lemon wedges and flake salt.
✅ Livi · ✅ Bianco (GF)
Bluefish — Grilled
Bluefish is oily and strong-flavored. It needs aggressive treatment. Hit it hard with lemon, garlic, oregano, olive oil. Don't overcook — it dries out fast. Serve with a salsa verde (parsley, capers, anchovy, garlic, olive oil — blitz while it rests).
The lemon cuts the oiliness. Don't skip it.
✅ Livi · ✅ Bianco (GF)
Simple Grilled Filets
If we end up with a mix of filets. EVOO, salt, pepper, lemon. 3-4 minutes per side. Salsa verde on the side.
✅ Livi · ✅ Bianco (GF)
🐟 Fish Market Backup —
Seared Tuna Steaks
Pat bone dry. Season hard with salt and pepper. Screaming hot cast iron — 90 seconds per side. Slice against the grain, fan on a platter. Serve with a citrus-soy dipping sauce or salsa verde.
✅ Livi · ✅ Bianco (GF)
Sea Scallops al Forno
Giant sea scallops, patted bone dry, seasoned hard. Screaming hot cast iron on the oven floor — 90 seconds per side. That crust is impossible anywhere else. Lemon butter finish (olive oil for Sarah).
